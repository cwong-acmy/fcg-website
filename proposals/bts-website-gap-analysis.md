## Section 1 — Navigation Structure

**Context:** Denzel listed the website's key content areas at 00:48:49 as: *"RP, sourcing in China, supply in China, FP, global sourcing."* Mapping these against the current nav reveals two problems: (1) items Denzel considers distinct sellable offerings are missing from the Solutions dropdown entirely, and (2) Whitelabel is duplicated across both Products and Solutions, creating confusion about what it is.

The Products dropdown covers APIs and platforms (things you integrate or build on). Solutions should cover use-case / outcome-oriented pages — the "what do you get" story rather than "what do you connect to." The China-facing solutions (Sourcing in China, Global Sourcing, Payment to China, Property Hosting) are all outcome stories and belong in Solutions.

## Section 3 — New Page: BTS Landing Page

**Context:** Denzel reviewed the current website and said *"我看的时候都不知道你要看什么"* (when I look at it I don't know what I'm supposed to be looking at) — 00:50:33. The core decision that followed: the China supply story should not live inside the main product website. It should be a dedicated landing page built specifically for the BTS London event (Jun 24–25), where the audience on Day 1 is TMC clients and Day 2 is corporate clients.

The main website stays product-focused (APIs, platforms, technical specs). The BTS landing page is a sales-focused one-pager that tells the China story cleanly without competing with G-Link docs or the Whitelabel pitch.

**Audience:** European TMCs and DMCs who currently book Chinese hotels through GDS and either (a) can't access local Chinese chains at all, or (b) can access international brands (Marriott, IHG) but not the local brands their travellers actually prefer — 亚朵, 全季, 锦江 etc. Elaine confirmed at 00:54:14: *"They prefer local brands because they already have the international ones."*

**Why this audience specifically:** Denzel gave the concrete example of Wins TMC at 00:51:33 — *"They came to us because they discovered we could give them 亚朵 and 全季, which they couldn't get anywhere else."* That is the proof point. The landing page should open with a version of that story.

## Section 4 — New Page: Payment Page

**Context:** 吴晓文 identified Payment to China as *"a very strong angle to sell to European clients and European DMCs"* at 00:49:38 — because European companies booking Chinese hotels have no clean settlement path today. The payment partner (Conferma) has been signed and confirmed. Elaine asked whether to name them; Denzel said yes, it's done.

Denzel was explicit about the page structure at 00:50:04: *"Create a new page. Two sections — front and back. Don't put it on one page. Don't confuse it, don't take away from the China supply page."* Payment is its own pitch to a slightly different buyer concern and should not share space with the hotel supply story.

**Target audience:** European TMCs and DMCs who need to pay Chinese hotels in RMB — currently they either absorb FX costs, rely on the hotel to invoice in USD/EUR (unreliable), or avoid certain Chinese properties altogether. FCG's payment service removes that friction entirely.
