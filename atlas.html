Inside the FCG stack

The mid-end layer, between booking and supply.

AtlasOS is the mid-end layer of the FCG stack, running on the ADDN network. Bookings flow in from the front end; AtlasOS is where the travel team handles the work that happens after — the messages, the service requests, the reconciliation, and the people in the field.

→

One queue for every channel

Traveller messages over Email, WhatsApp, WeChat, and SMS arrive in the same inbox, tied to the booking they belong to.

→

Cases that escalate on time

Service requests carry escalation workflows and SLA tracking, so nothing sits unattended and breaches are visible before they happen.

→

Reconciliation that ties back

Reverse-PNR look-up matches finance records to the booking behind them, turning a slow month-end into a clean one.
