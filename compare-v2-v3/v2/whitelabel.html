What's Included

Four layers. One branded travel product.

Everything you need to offer flights, hotels, and ground transport — without building the infrastructure yourself.

Layer 01

Branded Booking

A fully branded booking experience your customers see as yours. Flights, hotels, and ground transport in one interface — your logo, your colors, your domain.

  • Full visual customization — logo, color, typography, domain
  • Multi-product search: flights, hotels, ground transport
  • Mobile-responsive, consumer-grade booking flow
Layer 02

Supply Connected

Every search connects to G-Link and F-Link — FCG's hotel and flight supply layer. 1M+ properties. Every major airline. Deduplicated, normalized, commercially ready.

  • G-Link: GDS, bedbank, and direct hotel content in one feed
  • F-Link: GDS, NDC, and LCC direct in one standardized feed
  • Deep APAC supply — not retrofitted from a Western platform
Layer 03

Payment Infrastructure

The financial layer handles invoice capture, booking reconciliation, tax recovery, and settlement. The part of travel that looks simple until you try to build it.

  • Invoice ingestion and AI classification
  • VAT/GST recovery across jurisdictions
  • ERP integration for accounting workflows
Layer 04

Launch & Scale

Go live in weeks, not quarters. Zero engineering maintenance on your side. FCG handles supply updates, content quality, and platform uptime. You handle the brand and the customers.

  • Weeks to launch — not months of engineering
  • Ongoing supply and content maintenance by FCG
  • Scale to new markets without new integrations
