**Fusion Connect Group (FCG)** — next-generation global business travel & payment network. "Started in China, now global, with China still the core strength." Source deck: `FCG_Company_Deckpptx.pptx` (Company Introduction 2026, 27 slides) in this project folder. Contact: hello@fusionconnectgroup.com · fusionconnectgroup.com · open API at open.fusionconnectgroup.com.

**Thesis:** Rebuilding the rails of global business travel. Corporate travel still runs on decades-old centralized GDS infrastructure (intermediated, costly, rigid). FCG is the modern alternative — a direct, AI-powered, decentralized booking + payment network connecting buyers and suppliers worldwide. Three pillars: Direct & Decentralized (no middleman/fees), AI-Native (intelligence across sourcing→booking→servicing→settlement), Borderless (two-way: China outbound + global inbound on one network).

**Headline numbers:** 13+ yrs in corporate travel · USD 3B+ cumulative platform GMV · 4.35M+ global hotel records (1.35M+ China, 35K+ direct China independents) · 220+ countries · 50+ enterprise clients (central SOEs & Fortune Global 500) · #1 China negotiated-hotel scale · profitable through-cycle.

**History:** Founded 2013 Shenzhen (hotel supply-chain, direct contracting & RFP) → 2015 Titan Cloud SaaS → 2016 connected Ctrip & Meituan, GMV >USD 150M → 2017 integrated Top 60 hotel groups → 2019 AMT corporate-travel brand → 2021 COVID-resilient, revenue 300% above 2019 → 2023 flight supply / F-Link (GDS, NDC, LCC) → 2025 Fusion Connect Group global brands / worldwide distributed supply.

**Footprint:** HQ Hong Kong (Global HQ). Shenzhen = China HQ & Tech Centre. Servicing: Kuala Lumpur (global), Wuhan (China). Commercial offices: Beijing, Shanghai, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, London. Notable clients: State Grid, China Mobile, Ping An.

**Market case ("why now"):** Global business travel spend USD 1.57T (2025), USD 2T+ by 2029 (~7% CAGR). China = #2 market (USD 373B); China outbound ~USD 280B / ~145M trips/yr (world's largest source market); China inbound USD 135B, up ~15% YoY. The wedge: no one bridges China both directions compliantly — that gap is FCG's. Tailwinds: China travel rebounding, GDS lock breaking (NDC pressure), AI+cloud reset the cost base (Navan IPO, Spotnana proved the model). Incumbents distracted by consolidation (Amex GBT–CWT, Sabre, Amadeus).

**Positioning vs others:** Western platforms (Spotnana, GDS) = global but can't transact compliantly into China; China OTAs (Trip.com) = deep in China but no own global network; new TMCs (Navan, TravelPerk) = demand-side apps on others' supply. FCG = owns its network, serves two-way China + scales worldwide.

**Strategy "In China · Out China":** China as central hub of a two-way global accommodation/distribution/cross-border-payment network. OUT = connect Chinese multinationals/SOEs to global supply + cross-border payment. IN = open standardized China hotel supply + compliant local payment to overseas TMCs/agencies/foreign travelers.

**Core tech — ADDN (AI-Powered Decentralized Distribution Network):** industry-first, independently deployable travel+payment infrastructure replacing the centralized GDS core. Cloud-native, runs across many independent hubs (no central failure point); demand connects straight to supply; full-chain AI. Live across 3 hubs today — Hong Kong, Singapore, China (Wuhan) — expansion-ready (any region/country joins on demand). Result: low cost, high efficiency, fully compliant, end-to-end traceable, globally replicable. Moat compounds with each hub/client/transaction.

**Product stack (modular, all deployable on ADDN):**
- Front-end: Corporate Booking Tool (CBT, white-label) · AI Booking Tool (natural-language/voice booking, AI trip planning).
- Mid-end = **Atlas TMC OS**: OpsHub, consolidated omni-channel Inbox (Email/WhatsApp/WeChat/SMS), Finance & reconciliation (reverse-PNR), Duty of Care (live traveller map, risk feed), Reports.
- Back-end supply: **G-Link** (hotel aggregator — GDS/Amadeus & Sabre, corporate rates, 3rd-party, Amadeus Value Hotels) · **F-Link** (flight aggregator — GDS, NDC, LCC; pre-validation for booking stability). GDS rate custody loads corporate rates directly into Amadeus & Sabre.
- Services wrap: **AgreeEase** (RFP/procurement — full lifecycle, AI rate monitoring, rate custody, audit trails) · **Cross-Border Payments** (with Conferma — global-to-China VCC; >70% market VCC-to-China failure rate is the gap they close; GDS billing, traveller incidentals) · API / Developer Platform.

**Ultimate goal:** Become the default decentralized, AI-native, borderless network for global business travel & payments — displacing centralized GDS. Today: 3 live hubs → Next: multi-region network (hubs join on demand, data residency) → Ultimate: the global default.
