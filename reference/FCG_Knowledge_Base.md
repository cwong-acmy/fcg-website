## What FCG is
A next-generation, decentralized global **business-travel + payment network**. Started in China (2013, Shenzhen), now global, with China still the core strength. Tagline: **"In China · Out China"** — one two-way network.

## Thesis
Corporate travel still runs on decades-old centralized GDS infrastructure — intermediated, costly, rigid. FCG is the modern alternative: a direct, AI-powered, decentralized booking + payment network connecting buyers and suppliers worldwide. Three pillars:
- **Direct & Decentralized** — buyers connect straight to suppliers, no middleman/fees, no central point of failure.
- **AI-Native** — intelligence across the full chain: sourcing → booking → servicing → settlement.
- **Borderless** — two-way by design: China outbound + global inbound on one network.

## Competitive positioning
- **Western platforms (Spotnana, legacy GDS):** global, but can't transact compliantly into China
- **China OTAs (Trip.com):** deep in China, but no global network of their own
- **New TMCs (Navan, TravelPerk):** demand-side apps relying on others' supply
- **FCG:** owns its own network, serves two-way China travel, scales worldwide

## Strategy — "In China · Out China"
China as the central hub of a two-way global accommodation / distribution / cross-border-payment network.
- **OUT China** — connect Chinese multinationals & SOEs to global hotel supply + cross-border payment; standardized overseas travel management; unified cost control & compliance.
- **IN China** — open standardized China hotel supply + localized compliant payment to overseas TMCs, international agencies, and foreign business travelers.

## Core technology — ADDN
**AI-Powered Decentralized Distribution Network** — industry-first, independently deployable travel + payment infrastructure that replaces the centralized GDS core.
- Always on, scales on demand — cloud-native, runs across many independent hubs, no central system to overload/crash
- No middleman, no markup — demand connects straight to supply
- Full-chain AI — sourcing, mapping, booking, servicing, settlement
- **Live network:** 3 hubs today — Hong Kong, Singapore, China (Wuhan) — any region/country can join on demand
- Each hub runs the full system autonomously; hubs share only what a booking needs (data stays in-region); resilient to single-hub failure
- **Result:** low cost · high efficiency · fully compliant · end-to-end traceable · globally replicable
- **ADDN vs legacy GDS:** cloud-native vs monolithic · direct vs intermediated · transparent low-cost vs layered fees · modular vs rigid · China-compliant two-way vs one-way · AI built-in vs bolted-on

## Product stack (modular — all deployable on ADDN)
**Front-end (booking):**
- **Corporate Booking Tool (CBT)** — white-label booking for TMCs/resellers/partners; own domain; policy & approval workflows; fast go-to-market
- **AI Booking Tool** — natural-language chat + voice booking, AI trip planning & recommendations, connected to FCG's full content stack

**Mid-end — Atlas (TMC Operating System):**
- Consolidated omni-channel **Inbox** (Email, WhatsApp, WeChat, SMS)
- **Operations Hub** (service requests per booking, escalation, SLA tracking)
- **Finance & Reconciliation** (reporting, reverse-PNR lookup)
- **Duty of Care** (live traveller map, world-events feed, risk policy, AI next-step)
- Reports

**Back-end (supply):**
- **G-Link** — hotel supply aggregator (GDS/Amadeus & Sabre, negotiated/custody corporate rates, 3rd-party OTA/wholesaler, Amadeus Value Hotels) into one unified inventory
- **F-Link** — flight supply aggregator (GDS, NDC, LCC); pre-validation reduces booking failures
- GDS rate custody — corporate hotel & flight rates loaded directly into Amadeus & Sabre

**Services wrap:**
- **AgreeEase** — RFP & procurement (full lifecycle, AI rate monitoring, global rate custody, audit trails)
- **Cross-Border Payments** — with **Conferma**; global-to-China VCC (pay any China hotel by virtual card, incl. local/regional chains); GDS billing; traveller incidentals settlement. Closes the **>70% market VCC-to-China failure rate**
- **API / Developer Platform** — open.fusionconnectgroup.com

## Ultimate goal
Become the **default network for global business travel & payments** — displacing the centralized GDS model. Roadmap: **Today** = 3 live hubs running two-way network → **Next** = multi-region network (hubs join on demand, data residency) → **Ultimate** = the global default — decentralized, AI-native, borderless.
