Supply layer

One API. Four supply paths.

G‑Link connects the hotel paths a TMC needs: GDS, direct hotel, bedbank, and Asia-direct. Corporate rates, mapped content, and post-booking events run through one layer.

4.3M+ mapped hotels

GDS, direct hotel, bedbank, and Asia-direct supply are exposed as one bookable feed. Mapping, currency, and content reconciliation sit behind the API.

Corporate rates, not just inventory

RFP-negotiated rates flow through GDS and direct hotel. Wholesalers cannot carry those rates. For large corporates, that is roughly 70% of booking volume.

Asia depth around global GDS

G‑Link combines Chinese hotel chains with Sabre, Amadeus, Travelport, direct hotel, and channel-manager connections. Built from inside the region, not added later.

Mid-office handling after booking

Cancellations, holds, reschedules, and traveler emergencies run through the BMS. Direct-where-possible routing keeps accountability traceable.

Quality-scored sources

Supplier paths are ranked by confirmation reliability, cancellation success, and price stability. Bookings route through the most dependable path.

Supply · Buckets

Four supply paths.

Each path plays a different role. Together, they give TMCs corporate rates, regional depth, long-tail coverage, and one accountable layer.

A

Corporate-rate GDS

RFP-negotiated rates do not pass through wholesale. Direct GDS integrations carry the rate types large corporates need.

Evaluation

What TMCs ask first.

"We already use another wholesaler."

Wholesalers cannot carry corporate-negotiated rates.

RFP rates only flow through GDS and direct hotel. For a large corporate, that is roughly 70% of booking volume unreachable through wholesale. G‑Link adds the corporate-rate layer wholesalers structurally cannot deliver.

"We already use another travel platform."

Most large TMCs already multiplex APIs.

If China sits in your client mix, your current API may have a gap G‑Link is built to close. G‑Link combines Chinese hotel chains with Sabre, Amadeus, Travelport, direct hotel, and channel-manager connections. China is 58% of Asia travel and no global player has comparable depth.
