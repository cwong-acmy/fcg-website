What's Underneath

Four layers. One connection to real supply.

A connection between your AI and APAC-native travel supply: G-Link and F-Link inventory, agent-ready API objects, persistent session context, policy logic, and confirmed bookings.

Input

Language Understanding

The front door. Freeform traveler requests become structured, low-noise queries that hit G-Link and F-Link supply directly.

  • Text and voice input with multi-turn conversational context
  • Intent extraction mapped to bookable inventory parameters and policy constraints
  • Agent-ready response objects designed for tool calls, retries, and grounded recommendations
Supply: The Core

G-Link & F-Link Inventory

This is the layer that makes everything else valuable. 1M+ deduplicated hotel properties from GDS, bedbanks, and direct sources. Every major airline via GDS, NDC, and LCC direct. APAC-native supply depth. Real-time, not cached.

  • G-Link: multi-source hotel content, property-level and room-level deduplication, negotiated-rate layering
  • F-Link: fare-family normalization, ancillary management, full commercial control across every airline
  • Live rates, real availability, and confirmed bookings through one connection
Governance

Policy Engine

Policy is applied at the supply layer before options from G-Link and F-Link reach the traveler. The AI can explain trade-offs, request approval, or hand off with full context.

  • Budget caps, preferred suppliers, and class-of-service rules enforced at point of search
  • Pre-trip approval routing and out-of-policy flagging before the booking is made
  • Client-level and traveler-level rule layering across G-Link and F-Link results, with escalation context preserved
Output

Confirmed Bookings

The end result: complete, bookable itineraries with confirmed pricing from real supply. Not suggestions. Not cached results.

  • Multi-segment itineraries assembled from a single conversation against live G-Link and F-Link supply
  • Real-time pricing with supplier and rate source visibility across every connected source
  • One-step booking confirmation with no manual re-entry or downstream verification
