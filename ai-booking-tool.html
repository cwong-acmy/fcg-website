What's Underneath

Four layers behind every AI booking.

The experience feels like a chat assistant. Underneath, it is a managed booking platform with live supply, policy controls, booking records, approvals, and support context.

Input

Conversational Search

The front door. Freeform traveler requests become structured search, booking, change, and support actions that the platform can verify and complete.

  • Text and voice input with multi-turn conversational context
  • Intent extraction mapped to dates, destination, budget, traveler profile, and policy constraints
  • Grounded recommendations with clear reasons, trade-offs, and next-step actions
Supply: The Core

Live Travel Inventory

This is the layer that makes the assistant useful. 4.3M+ deduplicated hotels and 24.9M+ room types from GDS, bedbanks, and direct sources. Every major airline via GDS, NDC, and LCC direct. APAC-native supply depth. Real-time, not cached.

  • G-Link: multi-source hotel content, property-level and room-level deduplication, negotiated-rate layering
  • F-Link: fare-family normalization, ancillary management, full commercial control across every airline
  • Live rates, real availability, and confirmed bookings through one connection
Governance

Policy Engine

Policy is applied before options reach the traveler. The AI can explain trade-offs, request approval, or hand off to an agent with the full conversation and booking context.

  • Budget caps, preferred suppliers, and class-of-service rules enforced at point of search
  • Pre-trip approval routing and out-of-policy flagging before the booking is made
  • Client-level and traveler-level rule layering, with escalation context preserved
Output

Bookings and Servicing

The end result: complete, bookable itineraries with confirmed pricing from real supply, plus the records and context needed for changes, reporting, support, and payment workflows.

  • Multi-segment itineraries assembled from a single conversation against live supply
  • Real-time pricing with supplier and rate source visibility across every connected source
  • One-step booking confirmation with no manual re-entry or downstream verification
