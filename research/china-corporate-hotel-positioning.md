## 3. Five capability blocks (Connect · Procure · Manage · Pay)

### Block 1 — Domestic Chain & Standalone Direct Connect
Official PMS linkage with Top 100 China local hotel groups including **Huazhu, Atour, Jinjiang, BTG Homeinn**, plus exclusive contracts with **200,000 independent properties** across nearly **3,000 cities** in China. Secure exclusive corporate net rates free from OTA markup fees.

### Block 2 — G-Link API Gateway
One single integration unlocks **500,000+ live China hotel inventory** with real-time rate & availability synchronization and fully deduplicated resource data.

### Block 3 — AI-driven RFP & Centralized Procurement via AgreeEase
AgreeEase is China's leading AI-powered corporate hotel RFP tender platform. Takes over the full China hotel sourcing workflow: launch RFPs, invite targeted chain and standalone hotels to bid, run online negotiations, benchmark pricing via big-data analysis, lock exclusive corporate net rates automatically. Replaces manual spreadsheets, shortens procurement cycles from months to weeks, optimizes the hotel portfolio annually for a steady **12%–20% cut in accommodation spend**.

### Block 4 — Full Agreement Portfolio Custody & Ongoing Management
Full outsourced custody for all China hotel negotiated contracts: centralized rate archive, real-time pricing and compliance monitoring, proactive replacement of underperforming hotels, dynamic resource updates across all China city tiers. No in-house local admin team required at global HQ.

### Block 5 — China-compliant Auto-Pay End-to-End Settlement
Proprietary **Auto-Pay**: centralized RMB corporate payment, automatic Chinese VAT e-invoice issuance, bulk direct settlement with hotels, zero out-of-pocket prepayment for travelling staff. Compliant with China taxation and FX regulations, aligns with global corporate audit standards, eliminates manual reimbursement and cross-border reconciliation costs.
