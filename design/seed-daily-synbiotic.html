Questions? We're here to help.

When will I feel something? +
It is normal to feel effects in the digestive tract in as little as 24-48 hours. Other longer term benefits like cardiovascular health and gut immune function may take longer. As long as you continue your daily intake, those beneficial microbes are doing their work inside to program, sustain, and enhance your health.
How do I take DS-01® Daily Synbiotic? +
Adults—take 2 capsules daily, all at once. You can take DS-01 with or without food and maintain the efficacy of the product. However, you know your body best—find the best time of day to take for you.
Why should I take DS-01® Daily Synbiotic? +
You are home to a community of trillions of microorganisms, including 38,000,000,000,000 bacteria that live in and on you. They perform critical functions like digesting food, managing inflammation, and synthesizing key vitamins, metabolites and neurotransmitters. This is the microbiome. By supplementing your gut microbial makeup, DS-01® provides benefits for both digestive and whole body health.
