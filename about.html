Our story

From a Shenzhen supply chain to a global network.

Founded in 2013, FCG grew from direct hotel contracting into a full-stack, two-way travel and payment network — and stayed profitable through the cycle.

2013

Founded in Shenzhen — hotel supply chain, direct contracting and RFP.

2015

Launched Titan Cloud SaaS — productising the supply platform.

2016

Connected Ctrip & Meituan — platform GMV passed USD 150M.

2017

Integrated the Top 60 hotel groups — deepening negotiated supply.

2019

Launched the AMT corporate-travel brand — moving up the stack.

2021

COVID-resilient — revenue 300% above 2019.

2023

Added flight supply / F-Link — GDS, NDC and LCC content.

2025

Became Fusion Connect Group — global brands, worldwide distributed supply.
