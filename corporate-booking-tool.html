Platform Capabilities

Everything a managed travel program needs.

Multi-modal booking, policy enforcement, merchandising controls, financial integration, loyalty, duty of care, and sustainability tracking in one platform built for complex corporate structures.

•

Multi-modal booking across flights, hotels, rail, and car rental

Core

One search interface covers every travel mode. Travelers build complete itineraries without switching tools or calling their agent. NDC content, GDS fares, direct hotel rates, ground transport, add-ons, and preferred supplier offers appear in a single results view with policy compliance indicated on every option.

•

Multi-entity corporate structures

Config

Support regional HQs, subsidiaries, and branch offices with separate policies, approval chains, cost centers, and reporting hierarchies under one deployment.

•

White-label deployment for TMCs

Partner

TMCs deploy the booking tool under their own brand. Their corporate clients see the TMC identity. The TMC keeps the relationship, the data, and the margin.

•

Akrapay financial integration

Finance

Booking data flows directly into Akrapay for payment status, invoice reconciliation, VAT/GST recovery, refunds, settlement tracking, virtual card controls, and CO₂ reporting. Cost centers and GL codes are assigned at the moment of booking, not after.

•

Sustainability and carbon tracking

ESG

CO₂ emissions calculated per booking. Eco-certified properties flagged in search results. Carbon reports exportable by entity, department, or period for ESG compliance.

Fast Implementation

Go live in weeks, not quarters.

The Corporate Booking Tool is designed for rapid deployment. Your TMC configures policy rules, entity structures, and branding. Travelers start booking without a six-month migration project.

Policy configuration, not custom development

Rules, approval chains, rate caps, and traveler grades are configured through the admin interface. No code, no vendor dependency for changes.

Traveler profiles with zero re-entry

Import traveler data, loyalty numbers, document details, and preferences. Travelers update their own profiles. No manual data re-keying from the old system.

Connect to existing systems

SSO, HR feeds, expense tools, and ERP integrations. The booking tool fits into your existing corporate technology stack, not the other way around.

APAC-depth supplier coverage

Deep hotel inventory across Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, and Southeast Asia. Regional content that global platforms miss, powered by G-Link and F-Link.
